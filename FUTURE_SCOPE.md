# Future Scope

## Features simulated now

- Discovery from ten local fictional business fixtures
- Chennai-area schematic map and scan delay
- Transparent rule-based opportunity scoring
- Consent-first outreach and requirements conversations
- Tamil voice-note playback and transcription card
- Requirement extraction and verification status
- Controlled negotiation, deal summary and approval history
- Six-agent production activity and QA logs
- Responsive client website preview
- Deployment, invoice and payment status
- Post-launch maintenance request and change log

None of these actions contacts a real person, uploads data, creates a website, executes an AI model or processes money.

- Licensed business-data providers with permitted use terms
- Verified email, SMS or WhatsApp Business messaging providers
- Speech recognition and translation with consent and retention controls
- LLM providers with data-processing agreements and evaluation layers
- Object storage with malware scanning for customer drawings
- Payment gateway and accounting/tax services
- Contract/e-signature provider
- Domain registrar, DNS, hosting, observability and deployment platform
- Identity provider and tamper-evident audit storage

## Legal, consent and data-source considerations

- Confirm that every lead source permits commercial discovery and outreach.
- Apply purpose limitation, data minimisation, retention schedules and deletion workflows.
- Record the legal basis and owner consent before messaging or processing uploaded material.
- Identify AI assistants clearly and provide an immediate human escalation and opt-out path.
- Avoid inferring sensitive traits or business viability from public data.
- Protect customer drawings as confidential commercial material.
- Require human approval for prices, legal commitments, publishing and material scope changes.
- Comply with applicable Indian data-protection, telemarketing, tax, payment and consumer laws after professional review.
- Maintain immutable approval, content-source and deployment histories.
- Create a documented redress process for incorrect listings, unwanted contact and generated-content errors.
